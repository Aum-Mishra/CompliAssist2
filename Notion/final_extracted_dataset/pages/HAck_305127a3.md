## 💼 Project Name: SmartBudget Finance

### 🏢 Company Type

Personal Finance & Micro-Savings Company



### 📌 Project Overview

SmartBudget Finance is a small financial company that helps students and low-income individuals manage their daily expenses and save small amounts of money regularly.

### 🎯 Main Objective

To provide simple budgeting and savings services using a mobile app.

### 💰 Services Offered

1. Daily expense tracking
1. Monthly budget planning
1. Micro-savings (save ₹10–₹100 daily)
1. Basic financial advice
### 👥 Target Customers

- College students
- First-time earners
- Low-income workers
### 💻 Technology Used (Basic Level)

- Frontend: HTML, CSS
- Backend: Basic Python / PHP
- Database: MySQL
### 📈 Revenue Model

- Small monthly subscription (₹49–₹99)
- Commission from partner banks
