### Company type

Personal Finance & Micro-Savings company

### Project overview

SmartBudget Finance helps students and low-income individuals manage daily expenses and regularly save small amounts of money through a mobile app.

### Main objective

Provide simple budgeting and savings services using a mobile app.

### Services offered

- Daily expense tracking
- Monthly budget planning
- Micro-savings (₹10–₹100 daily)
- Basic financial advice
### Target customers

- College students
- First-time earners
- Low-income workers
### Technology (basic level)

- Frontend: HTML, CSS
- Backend: Basic Python / PHP
- Database: MySQL
### Revenue model

- Monthly subscription: ₹49–₹99
- Commission from partner banks
