## 1. Audit Objectives

To reconstruct any sequence of events leading to a content decision.

## 2. Logging Requirements

All systems must log:
*   User ID
*   Timestamp (UTC)
*   Action Type
*   Resource Accessed
*   IP Address

## 3. Retention Period

Logs are retained for 7 years to comply with financial service covenants.

## 4. Roles

### Roles and Responsibilities Matrix

The following roles are critical to the successful implementation of this policy:

#### Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
Overall owner of the policy enforcement and strategic alignment with global regulations.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### Data Protection Officer (DPO)
Responsible for ensuring all data handling procedures comply with GDPR, CCPA, and internal privacy standards.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### AI Ethics Lead
Oversees the deployment and tuning of AI models to prevent bias and ensure safety.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### Social Media Manager
Executes day-to-day engagement strategies in adherence to these guidelines.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### Moderation Lead
Supervises the tier-1 and tier-2 moderation teams, handling escalations.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### Security Operations Center (SOC)
Monitors for real-time threats and authorized access anomalies.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### Legal Counsel
Provides interpretation of regulatory updates and litigious risks.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### IT Infrastructure Head
Maintains the secure environment for data storage and processing.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### Audit Committee
Conducts quarterly reviews of process adherence and log integrity.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.

#### Human Resources
Manages disciplinary actions resulting from policy violations.

*   **Key Deliverables**: Weekly reports, incident logs.
*   **Accountability**: Direct line to the Executive Risk Committee.
