## 1. The Brand Safety Promise <a name='section-1'></a>

SocialLink ensures our clients never appear adjacent to terror, hate, pornographic, or controversial content. This is a contractual obligation.

## 2. GARM Alignment <a name='section-2'></a>

We adhere to the Global Alliance for Responsible Media (GARM) definitions.

*   **Hate Speech**: Aggression based on protected groups.
*   **Arms & Ammunition**: Sales or promotion of weapons.
*   **Adult Content**: Nudity or sexual explicit text.

## 3. Platform Specific Mandates <a name='section-3'></a>

### Reddit
*   **NSFW Filter**: Must be enabled at the account level.
*   **Subreddit Vetting**: Do not comment in subreddits with 'Quarantine' status or <1000 subscribers.

### Twitter/X
*   **Reply Controls**: Only reply to Verified accounts or accounts >6 months old.
*   **Topics**: Auto-hide keywords related to 'Politics', 'Religion', 'Cryptocurrency'.

### Discord
*   **Server Entry**: Only official partner servers. No 'Community' servers without VP approval.

## 4. The 'Red List' Keywords <a name='section-4'></a>

The following semantic categories trigger an automatic block:
*   Slurs (Racial, Ethnic, Gender)
*   Self-harm promotion
*   Extremist political slogans

## 5. Crisis Mode Protocols <a name='section-5'></a>

In the event of a national emergency or tragedy:
1.  **Stop-Loss**: Pause all scheduled content immediately.
2.  **Silence**: Do not post 'Thoughts and prayers' marketing.
3.  **Monitor**: Listen for brand mentions.
