## Decision Log

### CASE-2025-001
**Scenario**: A user on Twitter used the slang term 'Based' in response to a client post about sustainability.
**Conflict**: The term has origins in alt-right communities but has been co-opted by mainstream Gen-Z slang.
**Verdict**: **APPROVED** (Monitor)
**Rationale**: Contextual analysis showed the user profile was consistent with standard Gen-Z demographics, not extremist groups. The usage was complimentary. Blocking would alienate the target demographic.
**Policy Reference**: `Brand_Safety_Guidelines > Contextual Analysis`
---

### CASE-2025-004
**Scenario**: A 'Crypto Influencer' with 500k followers offered to promote our FinTech client for free.
**Conflict**: High reach opportunity vs. Risk of 'Pump and Dump' association.
**Verdict**: **REJECTED** (Hard Block)
**Rationale**: The influencer was found in the Restricted Entities Registry (Cluster: Crypto Scam). Association would violate SEC anti-touting laws.
**Policy Reference**: `Restricted_Entities_Registry > Financial Fraud`
---

### CASE-2025-015
**Scenario**: A Reddit user posted valid criticism of our product's downtime in r/Technology.
**Conflict**: Negative sentiment vs. Opportunity to show transparency.
**Verdict**: **ENGAGE** (Protocol: Correct Record)
**Rationale**: Silence implies guilt. A transparent, empathetic apology with a technical explanation (approved by Engineering) turns a negative into a trust-building moment. Do not be defensive.
**Policy Reference**: `Social_Risk_Assessment_SOP > L2 Response`
---

### CASE-2025-032
**Scenario**: AI Sentiment tool flagged the phrase 'This product is the bomb' as a Threat.
**Conflict**: Literal threat detention vs. Slang usage.
**Verdict**: **FALSE POSITIVE** (Override)
**Rationale**: Slang dictionary update required. Context is clearly positive.
**Policy Reference**: `AI_Usage_Policy > Human-in-the-Loop`
---

### CASE-2025-088
**Scenario**: Employee wanted to use ChatGPT Free to draft a tweet for a client.
**Conflict**: Efficiency vs. Data Security.
**Verdict**: **VIOLATION** (Warning Issued)
**Rationale**: Inputting client data into a public LLM trains the public model on our IP. Only Enterprise instances are allowed.
**Policy Reference**: `AI_Usage_Policy > Authorized Tooling`
